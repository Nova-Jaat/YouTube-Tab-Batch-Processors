2. Set up a free account on **v0.dev** or **Framer.com** (AI website generation).
3. Set up a free **Stripe Payment Link** account to collect payments instantly via debit/credit card.

### Days 3 – 5: The Prospecting Engine
1. Open Google Maps. Search your city or nearby towns for:
   - "Roofing contractors"
   - "Tree removal service"
   - "Auto detailing"
   - "Paving contractors"
2. Skip the top 3 paid Google ads. Scroll to businesses that have 4.5+ star reviews, but:
   - Their website is completely missing.
   - Or their website looks broken on mobile (no click-to-call button).
3. Add 40 business names, phone numbers, and owner email addresses into a free Google Sheet.

### Days 6 – 9: The 90-Second Loom Audit (Async Pitching)
1. For each prospect, spend 10 minutes in **v0.dev** or **Framer** generating a clean, modern 1-page mobile site featuring their company name and real Google reviews.
2. Record a 60-second Loom video:
   > *"Hi [Owner Name], saw your 5-star Google rating. I noticed that when customers visit your site from their smartphones, there isn't a direct tap-to-call button, which causes people to leave and call another contractor. I went ahead and mocked up a 2026 redesign for your business here [show preview on screen]. If you'd like to use this for your business, I can launch it on your domain today for $250. If not, no worries at all—have a great week!"*
3. Send 10 personalized Loom links per day via Email, Facebook Messenger, and SMS.

### Days 10 – 14: Closing & Instant Fulfillment
1. When they reply with *"Looks good, how does it work?"*, send your Stripe payment link for $250.
2. Connect their custom domain on Cloudflare or Vercel (takes 10 minutes).
3. **Immediate Upsell (Video #15):** Ask the owner:
   > *"Do you have a list of past customers from the last year or two who haven't used your service recently? If you give me the phone numbers, I will send them a quick text to reactivate them for a 20% cut of any new jobs booked."*
