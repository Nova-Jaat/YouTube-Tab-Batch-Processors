## 🎥 Phase 2: Content Reverse-Engineering & Production (Days 4–5)

### 1. Competitor Scraping & Script Extraction
1. Open **ViralHunter.app** $\rightarrow$ Select **Health** bucket $\rightarrow$ Sort by **Views**.
2. Find 3 proven reels with >100,000 views (e.g., *"Rating Costco Gut Health Items as a Nutritionist"* or *"Day 1 vs Day 30 Probiotic transformation"*).
3. Download the video and upload to **Descript.com** to automatically extract the verbatim transcript.

### 2. Scene Breakdown Prompt (Run in ChatGPT)
```text
Here is a viral transcript from a health creator:
[PASTE DESCRIPT TRANSCRIPT HERE]

I want to recreate this video scene-by-scene using my own AI male nutritionist avatar.
Please:
1. Divide this script into 3-4 distinct scenes (Hook, Item 1, Item 2, and CTA).
2. For each scene, write an exact image generation prompt showing my avatar in that setting holding or pointing to the item.
3. For each scene, write an Image-to-Video motion prompt in a dynamic UGC selfie/talking-head style.
```

### 3. Image-to-Video Animation in OpenArt.ai (Google Veo 3.1)
1. Go to **OpenArt.ai** $\rightarrow$ Navigate to **Video Generation**.
2. Select the **Google Veo 3.1** model.
3. Upload your generated avatar scene still as the reference image.
4. Set output aspect ratio to **9:16 vertical (1080p)**.
5. Paste the motion prompt:
   ```text
   UGC style talking head selfie video, the male nutritionist looks directly into camera speaking naturally with subtle head movements, pointing to the product on the shelf, realistic mouth movements and natural micro-expressions, 1080p high quality vertical video.
   ```
6. Click **Generate** and download the MP4 clip for each scene.

### 4. Assembly & Polishing in CapCut
1. Import all rendered clips into **CapCut Desktop / Mobile**.
2. **Speed Tuning:** If the AI speech is slightly rushed or sluggish, apply a **0.9x – 1.1x speed curve** for crisp conversational pacing.
3. **On-Screen Ratings:** Add bold rating graphics during product breakdowns:
   - `1. Kirkland Chia Seeds: 9/10`
   - `2. Frozen Wild Blueberries: 8/10`
   - `3. Probiotic Formula: 10/10 (My daily secret)`
4. **Dynamic Captions:** Auto-generate captions $\rightarrow$ Apply bold font (The Bold Font or Montserrat) with yellow/green keyword highlights.
5. **The Comment CTA:** Ensure the final 3 seconds display large text:
   > **Comment "ALIVE" & I'll DM you my exact morning gut protocol!**

## ⚠️ Troubleshooting & Algorithmic Optimization

* **Reels stuck below 300 views:** Your opening visual hook is too generic. Add motion in the first 0.5 seconds (e.g., avatar turning toward camera, holding up a bright red marker, or displaying bold contrast text like *"STOP drinking this if you're bloated"*).
* **High views, low comments:** Your CTA is buried. Ensure the trigger word (`ALIVE`) is displayed prominently on screen for at least the last 4 seconds of the video, and pinned in the top comment.
* **Many comments, low checkout conversions:** Inspect EverSell's chat logs. If prospects are hesitating on price, train EverSell to highlight the cost-per-day breakdown (*"It works out to just $0.50/day—less than a cup of black coffee"*).
