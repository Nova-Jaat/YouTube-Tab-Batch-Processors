# Next Steps Execution Playbook: Selling Websites to Contractors

**Reference Analysis:** [02_Selling_Websites_to_Contractors_Sam_Robinson.md](../video_analyses/02_Selling_Websites_to_Contractors_Sam_Robinson.md)  
**Creator:** Sam Robinson | **Model:** Local B2B Website Redesign Arbitrage

---

## 🎯 7-Day Sprint Goal
Find 40 local trade contractors with broken/ugly websites, send 25 personalized 90-second Loom audits, and close your first client for $350–$500 flat.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Equipment & Template Setup (Day 1)
1. **Loom:** Install the free Loom Chrome extension.
2. **Master Template:** Create a free account on Carrd.co ($19/yr Pro) or Framer (Free).
   - Build 1 universal contractor template with:
     - Prominent header with company name, license badge, and emergency phone number.
     - Large clickable button: `"Tap to Call: (XXX) XXX-XXXX"`.
     - 3 Service cards (e.g. Roof Replacement, Leak Repair, Free Inspection).
     - Customer reviews block with 5 gold stars.
     - 3-field contact form (Name, Phone, Service needed).
3. **Stripe:** Create a Stripe Payment Link for **$350** titled *"Contractor Mobile Website Redesign & Launch"*.

---

## 📝 Step 2: Exact Outreach Scripts
### 1. The Permission-First Email / Facebook Message
```text
Subject: quick question regarding [Business Name] website

Hey [Owner Name],

I was looking at local roofing contractors in [City] and noticed your website has a broken click-to-call button on iPhone and takes 6+ seconds to load, which is likely costing you quote requests every week.

I spent 15 minutes mocking up a clean, fast mobile version for your business. Made a quick 90-second video showing the design—mind if I send the link over?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

### 2. The 90-Second Loom Screen Recording Script
- **0:00 - 0:20:** Show their current site on an iPhone screen simulator. Highlight the broken phone link or tiny unreadable text.
- **0:20 - 0:50:** Switch tabs to your clean Carrd mockup. Show the bold "Tap to Call" button and customer review badges.
- **0:50 - 1:20:** *"If you like this and want it live on your domain, I can launch it this Friday for a flat $350. If not, feel free to keep the design ideas! Here is the checkout link if you'd like to get started: [Stripe Link]."*

---

## 📅 Step 3: Daily Execution Schedule
- **Day 2:** Search Google Maps for `"Roofers in [City]"`. Skip page 1. Add 30 contractors from pages 2–4 to your spreadsheet.
- **Day 3:** Send 30 permission-first emails.
- **Day 4:** When they reply *"Sure, send it over"*, record individual 90-second Looms and reply within 1 hour.
- **Day 5:** Follow up with all pending contacts: *"Hey [Name], just checking if you had a chance to view the 90-second mockup?"*
- **Day 6-7:** Collect $350 on Stripe, connect their domain via DNS, and offer a $49/mo maintenance & hosting retainer.
