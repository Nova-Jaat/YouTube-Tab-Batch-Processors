# Next Steps Execution Playbook: AI Creative Strategist for DTC Brands

**Reference Analysis:** [04_Is_AI_Taking_Over_Marketing_3_Madiots.md](../video_analyses/04_Is_AI_Taking_Over_Marketing_3_Madiots.md)  
**Creator:** 3 Madiots | **Model:** High-Volume Creative Angle Testing

---

## 🎯 7-Day Sprint Goal
Audit 3 scaling e-commerce brands on Meta, write 5 new contrarian hook angles with visual storyboards, and land 1 creative consulting retainer at $1,500/month.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Research & Angle Mining
1. Open the free **Meta Ad Library** (`facebook.com/ads/library`).
2. Search a category: *Skincare, Ergonomic Chairs, Functional Mushrooms, Dog supplements*.
3. Find brands running 20+ active ads. Look at their oldest running ads (these are their winners).
4. Identify the angle they have NOT tested:
   - *The Competitor Roast Angle*
   - *The 'My Doctor Told Me' Angle*
   - *The Extreme Stress-Test Demonstration Angle*

---

## 📝 Step 2: The Storyboard Deliverable
Create a 1-page Google Doc or Figma board with 3 columns:
1. **Visual Action (0-3s):** e.g., Extreme close-up of pouring black coffee into a white carpet.
2. **On-Screen Hook Text:** *"Why 85% of stain removers actually ruin your carpet fibers."*
3. **Spoken Voiceover Script:** Word-for-word copy designed for a UGC creator to read.

---

## 💼 Step 3: Outbound Outreach to Heads of Growth
```text
Hey [Name],

Huge fan of [Brand Name]. Saw you're running heavy Meta ads on [Product].

Noticed you guys haven't tested an extreme stress-test visual hook yet. I put together 3 ready-to-film creative concepts tailored for your customer demo that should drop your cost-per-click.

Here is the view-only Google Doc: [Link]. Feel free to test them with your team!
```
