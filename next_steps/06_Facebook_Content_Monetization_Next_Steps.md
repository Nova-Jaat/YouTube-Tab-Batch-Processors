---

## 🎯 30-Day Sprint Goal
Establish a US-targeted Facebook Page, achieve 60,000 eligible minutes viewed, and unlock the Performance Bonus program.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Page Setup & Geo-Signals
1. Create a Facebook Page categorized under **Creator / Entertainment**.
2. Profile & Banner: Professional typography, English-only bio.
3. Device settings: If posting from outside the US, ensure video captions, on-screen text, and audio tracks originate from US trending libraries.

---

## 📝 Step 2: Content Formula (3 Reels Daily)
- **Length:** 15 to 25 seconds (sweet spot for 100% completion rate).
- **Format:** High-curiosity everyday human interest, home organization transformations, or emotional storytelling.
- **Visuals:** Fast cuts every 2.5 seconds using CapCut. Large bold yellow subtitles with black stroke.
- **Posting Times:** 12:00 PM EST and 8:00 PM EST (peak US leisure hours).
