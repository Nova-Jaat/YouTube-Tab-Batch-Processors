# Next Steps Execution Playbook: High-Volume Ad Creative Production

**Reference Analysis:** [09_Ad_Creative_Strategy_100M_Sweat_Equity.md](../video_analyses/09_Ad_Creative_Strategy_100M_Sweat_Equity.md)  
**Creator:** Sweat Equity | **Model:** Modular 3-Second Hook Testing

---

## 🎯 7-Day Sprint Goal
Produce 15 modular video ad variations for a single product using 3 hooks × 2 bodies × 2 CTAs, and pitch a DTC brand for a $1,500 creative batch.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: The Modular Ad Architecture
- **Hook 1 (Problem):** *"If your dog constantly licks its paws, stop ignoring it."*
- **Hook 2 (Social Proof):** *"Over 50,000 pet owners switched to this chew in 2026."*
- **Hook 3 (Curiosity):** *"The 1 ingredient veterinarians say to avoid in dog food."*
- **Body 1 (Product Demo):** Shows chew texture, ingredients, and dog eating it.
- **Body 2 (Customer Unboxing):** Customer selfie video reviewing visible coat improvements.
- **CTA 1:** *"Get 20% off your first bag with code PUP20."*
- **CTA 2:** *"Tap below before stock runs out this week."*

---

## 🎬 Step 2: Assembly in CapCut Desktop
1. Import all hook clips and body assets into CapCut.
2. Export 12 unique video files named systematically: `Hook1_Body1_CTA1.mp4`, `Hook2_Body1_CTA1.mp4`, etc.
3. Package inside a clean Google Drive folder: `/Raw_Creatives/`, `/Edited_Ads/`, `/Thumbnails/`.
