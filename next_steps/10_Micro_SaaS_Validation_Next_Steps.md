# Next Steps Execution Playbook: B2B Micro-SaaS Customer Validation

**Reference Analysis:** [10_Sold_My_SaaS_For_Millions_Henry_SaaS.md](../video_analyses/10_Sold_My_SaaS_For_Millions_Henry_SaaS.md)  
**Creator:** Henry SaaS | **Model:** Pre-Selling B2B Workflow Tools Before Writing Code

---

## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Validate a specific B2B pain point, secure 3 paid pre-orders ($50 each), and specify an MVP before writing code.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: The Reddit & Community Discovery
1. Search Reddit: `r/bookkeeping`, `r/property观`, `r/realtors`.
2. Search queries: `"I hate doing this manually"`, `"Is there a tool that"`, `"Zapier broken"`.
3. Identify a repetitive manual task (e.g., extracting invoice numbers from vendor emails into QuickBooks).

---

## 📝 Step 2: The Validation Interview Script
Contact 20 professionals on LinkedIn or Reddit:
```text
Hey [Name],

I saw your comment about how frustrating it is to manually reconcile client expense receipts every Friday.

I'm an engineer designing a lightweight tool that connects Gmail receipts directly into QuickBooks with 1 click.

Would you be open to a 10-minute feedback call? I have nothing to sell—just want to make sure I build something that actually solves your headache.
```

---

## 💰 Step 3: The Pre-Sale Close
At the end of the call:
*"If I build this and have the beta ready in 3 weeks, would you be willing to lock in lifetime access for a one-time $99 beta deposit? If it doesn't save you 3 hours a week, I'll refund you immediately."*
**Rule: Do NOT write code until you have at least 3 paid deposits.**
