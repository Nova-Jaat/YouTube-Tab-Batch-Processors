# Next Steps Execution Playbook: The 'Rule of 100' Agency Outbound Engine

**Reference Analysis:** [12_14M_Agency_Owner_Honest_Advice_Cameron_England.md](../video_analyses/12_14M_Agency_Owner_Honest_Advice_Cameron_England.md)  
**Creator:** Cameron England | **Model:** Relentless Execution & 100 Daily Outreaches

---

## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Send 100 outreaches per day for 10 business days (1,000 total outreaches), book 8 discovery calls, and sign 1 agency retainer at $1,500/month.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Set Up the Zero-Cost CRM
Create a Google Sheet with columns:
`Company | Contact Person | Title | Email / Social | Date Outreached | Follow-up 1 | Follow-up 2 | Status | Notes`

---

## 📝 Step 2: The High-Volume Direct Message Template
Use across Instagram DMs, LinkedIn, and cold email:

```text
Hey [First Name],

Noticed you guys are handling [specific local service, e.g. residential roofing in Austin].

We run a simple speed-to-lead system that automatically texts quote leads within 30 seconds of form submission, which typically increases booked consultations by 35%.

Would you be open to a 2-minute video showing how it works? No pressure at all.
```

---

## ⏱️ Step 3: Daily Execution Routine
- **8:30 AM - 10:30 AM:** Prospect and log 50 new contacts into Google Sheets.
- **10:30 AM - 12:30 PM:** Send 50 personalized outreach messages.
- **2:00 PM - 3:30 PM:** Send Follow-Up #1 to all contacts from 48 hours ago.
- **3:30 PM - 5:00 PM:** Send 50 second-wave outreaches.
- **Strict Rule:** No reading articles, watching YouTube, or tweaking software until 100 daily outreaches are completed.
