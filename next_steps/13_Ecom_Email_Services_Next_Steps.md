## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Learn the 3 core Klaviyo email retention flows, build a demo account, and sell a 'Black Friday Email Strategy Setup' to a small Shopify brand for $500.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Klaviyo Demo Account Setup
1. Create a free Partner account at [Klaviyo.com](https://klaviyo.com).
2. Build 3 essential automated email flows:
   - **Abandoned Cart Flow (3 emails):** 1 hr (gentle reminder), 24 hrs (social proof & FAQ), 48 hrs (10% discount expiring).
   - **Welcome Series (4 emails):** Origin story, customer reviews, best-seller guide, VIP offer.
   - **VIP Early Access Early-Bird Flow:** Builds anticipation before holiday sales.

---

## 📝 Step 2: Pitching Small Shopify Brands
Search Instagram for indie e-commerce brands with 5k–30k followers. Subscribe to their newsletter. If you receive zero welcome emails in 24 hours, send this DM:

```text
Hey [Brand Name] team!

Love your products. I actually signed up for your newsletter yesterday but didn't receive an automated welcome sequence or discount confirmation.

Most brands lose 15-20% of first-time buyers due to missing automated flows. I specialize in setting up high-converting Klaviyo sequences.

Mind if I send over a 3-minute video showing the 3 core flows you should have live?
```
