## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Select 1 high-gravity ClickBank health/supplement offer, set up a Beacons.ai bridge page, and generate your first affiliate commission ($100+).

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Offer Selection on ClickBank
1. Log in to ClickBank Marketplace.
2. Filter: Category = **Health & Fitness** -> Sort by **Gravity: High to Low**.
3. Choose an offer with Gravity > 50 and Average $/Conversion > $120 (e.g. joint health, sleep support, blood sugar balance).
4. Generate your unique affiliate Hoplink.

---

## 🌐 Step 2: Build the Bridge Page (CRITICAL)
**Never post raw ClickBank links on social media.**
1. Create a free account on [Beacons.ai](https://beacons.ai).
2. Add a clean header: *"The Natural Nighttime Routine for Deep Rest & Energy"*.
3. Add a short 3-bullet summary of the benefits.
4. Add a button: `"Watch the Free Video Presentation"` linking to your ClickBank hoplink.

---

## 📹 Step 3: Organic Video Strategy
1. Post 2 reels daily on TikTok and Pinterest Idea Pins focusing on natural wellness tips.
2. CTA: *"Link in bio to see the exact nighttime ritual recommended by specialists."*
