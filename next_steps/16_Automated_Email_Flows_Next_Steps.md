## 🎯 7-Day Sprint Goal
Draft the 3 core email automation flows in Google Docs and pitch local clinics or e-commerce shops to set them up for $400 flat.

---

## 📝 Step 1: The 3 Core Email Sequences
1. **The 4-Part Welcome Series:**
   - Email 1 (Day 0): Immediate delivery of lead magnet/discount + founder greeting.
   - Email 2 (Day 1): The #1 mistake customers make before finding this solution.
   - Email 3 (Day 3): Real customer transformation case study.
   - Email 4 (Day 5): Direct invite to book a consultation or purchase best-seller.
2. **The Abandoned Cart / Intent Sequence:**
   - Email 1 (1 hour): "Forgot your cart?"
   - Email 2 (24 hours): Customer reviews addressing quality and shipping objections.
3. **The 60-Day Customer Reactivation:**
   - Subject: *"Quick question for you, [First Name]"* with a feedback poll or special return offer.
