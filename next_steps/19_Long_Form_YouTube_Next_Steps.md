## 🎯 30-Day Sprint Goal
Publish 8 long-form videos (8–10 minutes) in a high-RPM niche (Personal Finance or Psychology) using 100% free software.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Video Production Workflow
1. **Scripting with Claude (Free):**
   - Prompt: *"Write a 1,600-word educational YouTube video script on 'How to Build an Emergency Fund When Living Paycheck to Paycheck'. Include an open-loop hook in the first 45 seconds, 5 practical steps with real math, and a retention cliffhanger at minute 5."*
2. **Voiceover with CapCut AI (Free):**
   - Copy text into CapCut Desktop -> Apply natural narrator voice.
3. **Editing:**
   - Overlay 4K stock clips from Pexels and Pixabay.
   - Insert key stats using CapCut dynamic animated text presets.
4. **Thumbnail:**
   - Canva: High contrast, 3 bold words maximum, expressive human face on side.

---

## 📅 Step 2: 8-Week Upload Commitment
- Upload every Tuesday and Friday at 2:00 PM EST.
- Include 3 affiliate book links in the description of every video.
