## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Build a list of 200 verified B2B decision makers, send 30 personalized outreach touches daily, and sign 1 client at $1,000/month.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Prospect Sourcing on Apollo.io
1. Filter on Apollo.io (Free tier):
   - Location: United States / UK / Canada.
   - Industry: Digital Marketing Agencies OR Real Estate Brokerages.
   - Employee Headcount: 5 to 25 employees.
   - Job Title: Founder, Managing Partner, CEO.
2. Export 150 verified email addresses and LinkedIn profiles.
