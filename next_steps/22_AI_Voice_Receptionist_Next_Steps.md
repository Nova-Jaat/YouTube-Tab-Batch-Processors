## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Configure an inbound phone agent on Vapi.ai, buy a local Twilio phone number, and sell an 'After-Hours AI Receptionist' to a local clinic for $500 setup + $150/mo.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Configure Vapi.ai
1. Create an account on [Vapi.ai](https://vapi.ai).
2. Configure Assistant prompt:
   > *"You are Sarah, the virtual receptionist for Dr. Miller's Dental Practice. Be warm, professional, and concise. Your goal is to answer customer questions about hours, services, and emergency dental appointments. If the caller wants to book, ask for their name, phone number, and preferred day, then confirm you will pass it to the scheduling coordinator."*
3. Connect Twilio: Purchase a local area-code phone number ($1.15/month) and bind it to your Vapi assistant.

---

## 📞 Step 2: The Live Demonstration Pitch
Call the number yourself, record the conversation on speakerphone, and send the audio file to local dentists and plumbers:
*"Hey [Owner], 30% of emergency dental and plumbing calls happen after 6:00 PM when nobody is in the office. Here is a 45-second recording of our AI receptionist answering a call, taking patient details, and booking an estimate last night. We can deploy this on your business line for $500."*
