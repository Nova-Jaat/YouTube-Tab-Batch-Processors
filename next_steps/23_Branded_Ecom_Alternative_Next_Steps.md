## 🎯 Strategic Pivot for Beginners
**Do NOT spend $2,000 on dropshipping ads if you are a beginner with limited savings.**  
Instead, take the skills that successful dropshippers need (Shopify store design, product copywriting, and review apps) and sell them as a **Done-For-You Shopify Setup Service** to local retail businesses transitioning online.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: The Deliverable Package
- 1 Clean Shopify Store on a free modern theme (Dawn or Refresh).
- 5 Product listings with AI-written direct-response descriptions.
- Customer reviews widget (Judge.me free).
- Stripe & PayPal checkout configuration.
- Total fee: **$600 flat**.

---

## 💼 Step 2: Finding Clients
Walk into local boutique stores, jewelry shops, and artisan bakeries in your city:
*"Hey [Owner], love your products. Noticed you only sell in-person and don't have a direct online shipping store. I can set up your complete Shopify online store this weekend for $600."*
