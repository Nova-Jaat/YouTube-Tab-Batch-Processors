## 🎯 7-Day Sprint Goal
Set up your AI editing stack (Gling + CapCut), edit 1 free sample Short for a podcast creator, and sign a $600/month retainer for 8 shorts/month.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: The 3x Faster AI Workflow
1. **Rough Cut:** Run raw footage through **Gling.ai** to eliminate all pauses and filler words in 60 seconds.
2. **Dynamic Captions:** Import into CapCut Desktop -> Auto Captions -> Apply animated bounce style.
3. **B-Roll & Sound Effects:** Add whoosh transitions and relevant stock footage at 5-second intervals.
4. Total production time per Short: **Under 20 minutes**.

---

## 💼 Step 2: Pitching Business Podcasters
Find 10 podcasts on YouTube with great long-form episodes but poor short-form repurposing. Download 1 episode, create a viral 30-second Short, and email the host:
```text
Hey [Host],

Loved your recent episode with [Guest Name].

Noticed you guys aren't posting short-form clips to TikTok and YouTube Shorts. I took a 45-second highlight from your interview, cut the dead air, and added animated captions: [Link].

If you like it, feel free to post it on your channel! If you'd like 8 of these per month, I can handle them for $600/mo.
```
