## 🎯 30-Day Sprint Goal
Build a working Speed-to-Lead qualification workflow on Make.com + Claude API, send 50 outreaches daily, and sign 2 clients at $2,500 setup + $1,000/mo retainer.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Build the Core Workflow (Make.com)
1. **Trigger:** Form submitted on client website (Webhook).
2. **Claude API Call:** Prompts Claude to evaluate lead budget, timeline, and urgency:
   > *"Evaluate this incoming lead. If budget > $5,000, mark priority HIGH and draft a personalized SMS response addressing their specific project. If budget < $5,000, mark STANDARD."*
3. **SMS / Twilio Action:** Sends the drafted SMS to the prospect in under 30 seconds.
4. **CRM Action:** Adds lead record with AI summary into HubSpot / Google Sheets.
