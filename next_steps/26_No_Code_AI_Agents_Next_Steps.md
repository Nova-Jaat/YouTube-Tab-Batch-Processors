## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Deploy a visual AI chatbot on **Voiceflow** or **Flowise**, train it on a business's knowledge base PDF, and sell the website installation for $500.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Build in Voiceflow (Free Tier)
1. Create a project at [Voiceflow.com](https://voiceflow.com).
2. Knowledge Base: Upload a client's pricing sheet, policy document, or service FAQ PDF.
3. Design the conversation flow:
   - Greeting -> Intent identification -> Knowledge query -> If lead wants to book, trigger capture node (Name, Email, Phone).
4. Embed: Copy the 1-line `<script>` tag and paste into the client's Webflow/WordPress header.

---

## 💼 Step 2: Pitching Local Service Clinics
Show the live widget on your phone answering complex insurance and pricing questions:
*"Most website visitors leave if they can't find pricing or insurance answers in 10 seconds. This AI assistant answers their exact questions 24/7 and books appointments directly into your calendar. We can install it this week for $500."*
