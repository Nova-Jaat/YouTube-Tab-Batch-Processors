# Next Steps Execution Playbook: Async Video Selling (No Sales Calls)

**Reference Analysis:** [27_AI_Agency_30_Days_No_Sales_Calls_Adam_Erhart.md](../video_analyses/27_AI_Agency_30_Days_No_Sales_Calls_Adam_Erhart.md)  
**Creator:** Adam Erhart | **Model:** The Spear Email + Loom Async Close

---

## 🎯 7-Day Sprint Goal
Send 25 Spear Emails daily, record 3 personalized Looms daily for interested prospects, and close 1 client without ever getting on a live phone call.

---

## 📝 Step 1: The 2-Sentence Spear Email
```text
Hey [First Name],

Noticed your website checkout drops off on mobile and doesn't support Apple Pay.

I recorded a 90-second screen video showing how to fix it—mind if I send the link over?

Best,
[Your Name]
```

---

## 📹 Step 2: The 3-Minute Loom Structure
- **Minute 0-1:** Show their actual website. Point out the exact friction point on mobile.
- **Minute 1-2:** Show the solution (preview a clean mockup or demonstrate the one-click fix).
- **Minute 2-3:** *"If you want me to handle this for you, here is the direct Stripe checkout link: [Stripe Link]. I'll have it implemented and live by Friday. No sales calls needed."*
