---

## 🎯 7-Day Sprint Goal
Build a 4x4x3 ad creative matrix (4 hooks × 4 angles × 3 bodies), batch film/edit 16 ad variations in 1 day, and deliver to an e-commerce brand for $750.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: The Production Grid
Create a Google Sheet with columns:
`Ad ID | Hook Name | Hook Script | Body Angle | Body Footage | CTA Style | File Status`

- **Hook Types:** (1) Shocking Statistic, (2) Direct Question, (3) Common Myth Busted, (4) Before & After Reveal.
- **Body Angles:** (1) Feature Demonstration, (2) Customer Review Testimonial, (3) Unboxing & Texture.
- **CTAs:** (1) Limited Time 20% Off, (2) Buy 2 Get 1 Free, (3) 30-Day Money Back Guarantee.

---

## 🎬 Step 2: 1-Day Batch Execution
- **Morning (9 AM - 12 PM):** Write all 16 modular scripts and organize b-roll assets.
- **Afternoon (1 PM - 5 PM):** Edit on CapCut Desktop using duplicate project timelines. Export all 16 files.
