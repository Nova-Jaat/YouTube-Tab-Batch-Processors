## 🎯 14-Day Sprint Goal
Select 1 trending product with >15% commission on TikTok Shop, request free sample or buy 1 unit, post 3 native demonstration videos daily, and earn first commissions.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: Product Selection
1. Open TikTok Creator Center -> **TikTok Shop Affiliate Marketplace**.
2. Filter: Commission > 15%, High Daily Sales Velocity.
3. Categories that convert fast: Posture correctors, portable neck fans, car cleaning putty, aesthetic desk lamps.

---

## 📹 Step 2: The Authentic Demonstration Formula
**Do NOT use generic AI avatars (TikTok suppresses them).**
- Film with your phone in natural daylight:
  - **0-3s Hook:** Show the immediate problem (e.g. dirty car cup holder).
  - **3-10s Solution:** Show the product cleaning it in 1 swipe with satisfying sound.
  - **10-15s CTA:** *"Tap the yellow basket below before the flash sale ends."*
- Tag the exact product listing in the video settings.
