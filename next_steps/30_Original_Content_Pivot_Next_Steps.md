## 🎯 Strategic Pivot: Avoiding the Reused Content Ban
**Do NOT scrape raw CCTV footage and upload compilations.** YouTube's automated Content ID policy will permanently demonetize or ban your channel.

---

## 🛠️ The Legitimate 'Reaction & Analysis' Model
If you want to monetize viral clips safely:
1. **Add Face & Voice Commentary:** Film yourself reacting to the clip in the corner of the screen.
2. **Provide Transformative Value:** Add educational breakdowns (e.g., *"3 mistakes this homeowner made with their security camera placement"*).
3. **Fair Use Compliance:** Keep clips under 10 seconds and pause to provide spoken analysis.
