## 🎯 30-Day Certification Goal
Pass the AWS Certified Cloud Practitioner (CLF-C02) exam, update your resume with ATS-optimized keywords, and apply to 10 entry-level Cloud Support roles.

---

## 🛠️ Step 1: 30-Day Study Plan
- **Week 1:** Cloud Concepts & Global Infrastructure (Regions, Availability Zones, Edge Locations).
- **Week 2:** Core Services (EC2, S3, RDS, DynamoDB, Lambda, VPC).
- **Week 3:** Security & Pricing (IAM, Security Groups, Well-Architected Framework, Cost Explorer).
- **Week 4:** Take 5 practice exams on Udemy (aim for >85% consistently).

---

## 📄 Step 2: The ATS Resume Optimization Checklist
1. Format: Clean single-column `.docx` document.
2. Mandatory Keywords: *AWS Cloud Practitioner Certified, Amazon EC2, S3 Bucket Management, IAM Security Policies, CloudWatch Monitoring*.
3. Experience Bullet Example: *"Configured scalable AWS infrastructure utilizing S3 and CloudFront, reducing content delivery latency by 35%."*
