# Video 15: It’s Boring, But It’ll Make You More Money Than Anything Else in Marketing

- **Original Video Link:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBySJTvEz6Q](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tBySJTvEz6Q)
- **Primary Business Model:** Database Reactivation & Cold Lead Revival (Email/SMS B2B Service)
- **Target Audience:** Local businesses, medical spas, dentists, remodelers, auto detailers
- **Beginner Feasibility Rating:** 9.5/10 (Highest ROI-to-effort ratio in the entire industry)
- **Speed to First Dollar:** 3 – 7 Days
- **Startup Capital Required:** $0 – $20 (Twilio SMS credits or free email trial)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Premise
The video exposes the glamorous trap of modern marketing (complex TikTok campaigns, expensive Meta ads, viral algorithms) and reveals the most profitable, unsexy secret in the marketing industry: **Database Reactivation (DBR)**. Established businesses sit on spreadsheets containing 500 to 5,000 "dead" leads—people who inquired in the past 2 years but never bought, or past customers who stopped visiting. By sending a simple 9-word text or re-engagement email to this existing list, you can generate $5,000 to $25,000 in immediate sales for the business in 48 hours without spending a single dollar on ads.

---

## 2. The Famous "9-Word Email" Framework
Pioneered by marketing legend Dean Jackson, this framework produces astonishing conversion rates because it feels 100% human, casual, and personal:

```
Subject: [First Name]?
Body:
Are you still looking to get your [Service, e.g. roof replaced / teeth whitened / kitchen remodeled]?
```

When sent to 1,000 old leads, 150–250 people will reply with *"Yes, actually, we got busy earlier, how much would it be?"*

---

## 3. The Irresistible Revenue-Share Offer
Why this is the easiest service for a beginner to sell:
- You approach the business owner and say:
  > *"I don't want your money upfront. You have a list of past leads that cost you money to acquire and are sitting idle. Let me run a 48-hour reactivation campaign for your dormant leads. I will write the messages and handle the replies. If you make $0, you owe me $0. If we generate sales, I just take 25% of the gross profit."*
- **No business owner says no to this.** There is literally zero risk for them.

---

## 4. Technical Execution (Dead Simple)
1. Get their old customer/lead list exported as a `.csv` file (First Name, Phone Number, Email).
2. Clean the list (remove duplicates and bad numbers).
3. Import into an SMS/Email platform (GoHighLevel, MailerLite, or a Twilio campaign).
4. Fire the 9-word re-engagement message in small batches (50 per hour so their staff doesn't get overwhelmed by phone calls).

---

## 5. 7-Day Beginner Action Plan
- **Day 1:** Study the 9-word reactivation framework and prepare a simple 1-page proposal sheet.
- **Day 2:** Identify 10 local businesses that have been in operation for over 3 years (Chiropractors, Roofers, Landscapers, Car Detailers).
- **Day 3:** Walk in or call the owner: *"Hey Dr. Smith, do you have a list of past patients from the last 2 years who haven't booked an appointment recently?"*
- **Day 4:** Sign a simple 1-page revenue share agreement (20% to 30% of recovered revenue).
- **Day 5:** Receive their CSV file of 500 contacts; upload to a messaging tool.
- **Day 6:** Launch the reactivation campaign on Friday morning.
- **Day 7:** Tally the appointments booked and collect your commission check.
