# Video 22: $300k/m Ecom Store Proves Dropshipping Is NOT Dead! (Start Here)

- **Original Video Link:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ta3BYsDhaSI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ta3BYsDhaSI)
- **Primary Business Model:** Branded E-Commerce Dropshipping / One-Product Shopify Stores
- **Target Audience:** E-commerce aspirants, TikTok shop creators, dropshippers
- **Beginner Feasibility Rating:** 3.5/10 (High financial risk and steep learning curve for broke beginners)
- **Speed to First Dollar:** 14 – 30 Days (If an ad hits)
- **Startup Capital Required:** $500 – $1,500 (Shopify, product testing samples, paid ad spend)

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Core Premise
The video showcases a Shopify dropshipping store scaling to $300,000/month, refuting the widespread claim that "dropshipping is dead." The creator explains that *traditional* dropshipping (copy-pasting ugly AliExpress products with 4-week shipping times) is indeed dead. Modern profitable dropshipping functions like a legitimate micro-brand: selecting a single viral problem-solving product, working with private agents (e.g. CJ Dropshipping, Zendrop) for 6–8 day global delivery, creating custom aesthetic video ads, and running high-velocity TikTok paid ads.

---

## 2. The Modern Dropshipping Formula
1. **Product Selection Criteria:**
   - Must have a clear "Wow" factor visible in the first 2 seconds.
   - Solves an acute everyday frustration (e.g. back pain, pet shedding, car organization).
   - High perceived value: Can be bought from supplier for $8 and sold for $39.99 (75%+ gross margin).
2. **Branded One-Product Store:** Clean, minimalist Shopify theme with custom product photography, customer video reviews (Loox), and trusted payment icons.
3. **TikTok Organic & Paid Ads:** Creating 3–5 daily TikTok UGC videos showcasing the transformation.

---

## 3. The "Noob" Reality Check (Brutal Truth & Red Flags)
- **The Capital Drain:** Dropshipping is NOT a free business model. You will spend $300 to $800 in Facebook or TikTok ad spend testing 3 to 5 products before finding a winner. If you are broke, you risk losing your rent money.
- **Payment Gateway Holds:** Stripe and PayPal hold 10% to 20% of your funds for 90 days when a new store suddenly does thousands in daily volume.
- **Supplier Delays & Customer Chargebacks:** One batch of defective items or a 2-week shipping delay can destroy your merchant processing account.

---

## 4. Beginner Verdict
If you are a complete beginner with less than $1,000 in savings, **do not start with dropshipping**. Build cash flow through high-margin B2B services (Websites, Lead Gen, Reactivation) first, and only risk disposable capital on e-commerce later.
