## 2. Monetization Mechanics
- **YouTube Partner Program (AdSense):** High volume of views (5M+ views/month), but relatively low RPM ($1.00 – $2.50 per 1,000 views) due to broad, non-commercial entertainment audiences.
- **Facebook Watch / Reels Bonuses:** Repurposing compilations to Facebook pages where casual video consumption is massive among older demographics.
- **Brand Sponsorships & Gaming App Integrations:** Promoting mobile games (Raid Shadow Legends, Solitaire Cash) via 30-second mid-roll integrations once subscriber count surpasses 100k.

---

## 3. The "Noob" Reality Check (Brutal Truth & Red Flags)
- **The "Reused Content" Trap:** YouTube's monetization review team routinely rejects channels for monetization under the "Reused Content" policy if the video is merely a raw montage of other people's footage without significant transformative value.
- **To Get Monetized Legally, You Must:**
  1. Add an original, audible voiceover commentary explaining what happens in each clip.
  2. Add on-screen educational or comedic context and face reactions/analysis.
  3. Obtain explicit permission or license clips from agencies like Jukin Media, Newsflare, or ViralHog (which costs money).
- **Copyright Strikes:** Scraping viral videos from Reddit, Twitter, or TikTok without permission will inevitably lead to copyright strikes that can terminate your channel overnight.

---

## 4. Beginner Verdict
**Do not start here if you need fast, reliable income.** Compilation channels are an algorithmic grind filled with copyright legal risks. Focus on models where you control the asset and the revenue relationship (B2B services, digital tools, or affiliate content).
